    FAQ

    How it works.

    What happens when I pair two coins?

    rot.actor reads each coin's name, ticker and logo. The pair decides the character's body, eyes and hat, and the two logo colours become its main colour and accent. The 3D model is built and rendered in your browser, and an AI writes the name, ticker, lore and catchphrase. Nothing launches until you press Launch and sign.

    Do I need to hold the parent coins?

    No. Any two Solana coins work, including ones that have never touched rot.actor. Only their public name, ticker and logo are read.

    How does the launch work?

    The image and metadata are uploaded to pump.fun's IPFS. PumpPortal builds the create transaction, your browser generates the new mint key, and your wallet signs. The result is a normal pump.fun coin on its bonding curve.

    Who is the creator?

    You. It launches from your wallet, so pump.fun's creator fees go to you. rot.actor never holds your keys or your coins.

    What does it cost?

    pump.fun's launch costs (about 0.02 SOL), plus any dev buy you choose and PumpPortal's 0.5% fee on that buy. rot.actor adds no fee.

    How are the parents recorded?

    The launch transaction carries a memo with both parent mints, the new mint and the look code, plus a read-only rot.actor registry key. The bestiary and bloodlines are rebuilt from those transactions. There's no database.

    Do the parent coins get anything?

    No. They're named as parents on the child's pump.fun page and in its bloodline. No fees or tokens go to them.

    Is rot.actor part of pump.fun?

    No. rot.actor is an independent site that launches coins through pump.fun. Memecoins can go to zero, and nothing here is financial advice.
