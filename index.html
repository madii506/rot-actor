    FAQ

    Questions from the kitchen.

    What happens when I fuse two coins?

    Both coins go into the pot. The first decides the pasta body and dough colour, the second the eyes, hat and accent. An AI writes a sing-song Italian name, a ticker and a line for it to say. Nothing is launched until you press Launch and sign.

    Do I need to own the parent coins?

    No. Any two Solana coins work, even ones that never touched rot.actor. Your launch only reads their name, ticker and picture.

    What does it cost?

    pump.fun's own launch costs (about 0.02 SOL) plus any dev buy you choose. rot.actor charges nothing.

    Who is the creator of my brainrot?

    You. It launches from your wallet, so pump.fun's creator fees go to you. rot.actor never holds your keys or your coins.

    Do the parent coins earn anything?

    No. They get a child and a place in its bloodline on Solana, and their name on its pump.fun page. That's all.

    How does the bestiary know my coin?

    Every rot.actor launch carries a short note on Solana with both parents. The bestiary and the family trees are rebuilt from those notes, not from a database.

    Is the voice an AI?

    It's your browser's own speech voice, Italian when your device has one. Sound only plays after you turn it on or press a speak button.

    Is rot.actor part of pump.fun?

    No. rot.actor is an independent site that launches coins through pump.fun. Memecoins can go to zero, and nothing here is financial advice.
